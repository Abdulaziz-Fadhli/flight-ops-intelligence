# Passenger Rebooking Policy

## Scope

This policy governs rebooking options offered to passengers affected by
flight delays, gate changes, or cancellations on domestic and international
routes operated by the airline.

## Delay-Based Rebooking Thresholds

- **Delay under 2 hours**: no rebooking offered. Passengers wait for the
  original flight.
- **Delay 2-4 hours**: passengers may request a rebooking onto the next
  available flight on the same route at no additional cost, subject to seat
  availability.
- **Delay over 4 hours**: passengers are automatically eligible for
  rebooking onto the next available flight, including flights operated by
  partner airlines if no seats remain on the airline's own network within 6
  hours.
- **Delay over 8 hours or overnight**: passengers are eligible for
  rebooking plus a hotel accommodation voucher, subject to the Delay
  Compensation Policy.

## Cancellation-Based Rebooking

If a flight is cancelled, the passenger is automatically rebooked onto the
next available flight on the same route at no cost. Passengers may instead
request a full refund in place of rebooking; this must be requested within
30 days of the cancellation.

## Rebooking During Weather Holds

Rebooking requests during an active weather hold (see Weather Operations
Procedures) are queued and processed in the order flights are cleared for
departure, not in the order rebooking requests were submitted. This
prevents passengers from later flights displacing seats already needed by
passengers from earlier, still-delayed flights.

## Fare Difference Waivers

No fare difference is charged when rebooking is airline-initiated (delay,
cancellation, schedule change). If a passenger voluntarily requests a
different flight for personal convenience, standard fare difference and
change fee rules apply instead of this policy.
