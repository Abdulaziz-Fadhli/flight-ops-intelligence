# Flight Cancellation Policy

## Decision Authority

Only the duty operations manager or above may cancel a scheduled flight.
Cancellations must record a delay reason code explaining the underlying
cause (WEATHER, ATC, CREW, TECHNICAL, or LATE_INBOUND) even though the
flight's status itself is recorded as CANCELLED rather than DELAYED.

## Passenger Options on Cancellation

A cancelled flight's passengers are automatically offered, at no cost:

1. Rebooking onto the next available flight on the same route (see
   Rebooking Policy), or
2. A full refund to the original payment method, or
3. Rebooking onto a different route via a connection, if the direct route
   is cancelled for an extended period (multi-day weather event, for
   example).

## Notice Period Requirements

- Cancellations made more than 14 days before scheduled departure require
  only standard notification (email/app notification).
- Cancellations made within 14 days require proactive rebooking to be
  offered automatically rather than waiting for the passenger to request
  it.
- Cancellations made within 24 hours of scheduled departure require a
  direct phone or SMS notification attempt in addition to email/app
  notification.

## Same-Day Cancellation and Rebooking Interaction

If a flight is cancelled and then a replacement flight is added on the same
day and route, affected passengers should be rebooked onto that replacement
automatically before general rebooking rules are applied to the wider
schedule, since they were already holding a confirmed seat on the cancelled
flight.
